<h2>Your first little sentence</h2>
<div class="reading" lang="fa" dir="rtl">سام آب داد.</div><p class="gloss">Sâm âb dâd. · Sam gave water.</p><p>Read the person, the thing given, then the action. The full stop ends the sentence.</p><div class="task"><strong>Try 34.</strong> Write the words below in reading order to say “Sam gave water.”</div><p>Word bank: <span class="fa" lang="fa" dir="rtl">داد</span> · <span class="fa" lang="fa" dir="rtl">سام</span> · <span class="fa" lang="fa" dir="rtl">آب</span></p><div class="write-lines"><div></div><div></div><div></div></div><p class="note">Persian allows other constructions. This is one useful beginner pattern, not a rule for every sentence.</p>

<h2>Two new words: cold and is</h2>
<div class="reading" lang="fa" dir="rtl">سَرد &nbsp; اَست</div><p class="gloss">sard · cold &nbsp;&nbsp; ast · is</p><p>Sin carries short a in cold. Alef carries short a at the start of is.</p><p>There is no extra vowel between s and t in <i>ast</i>. We show the written form here; casual speech may sound different.</p><div class="task"><strong>Try 35.</strong> Match A <span class="fa" lang="fa" dir="rtl">سَرد</span> and B <span class="fa" lang="fa" dir="rtl">اَست</span> to is and cold.</div><div class="write-lines"><div></div><div></div></div>
