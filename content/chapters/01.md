<h2>A little book. A real beginning.</h2>
<p>Meet eight Persian letters. Discover how they connect. Follow Sam and Sara through a windy picnic, then read three short sentences about its ending.</p><p>You do not need to know Persian before you begin. Work slowly, and return to pages whenever you need them.</p><h3>Your goal</h3><p>By the end, recognise the eight letters taught here and read the book’s practised words and short sentences with vowel support.</p><p class="note">This is an introduction to reading, not a complete alphabet or conversation course. The story narration is in English; Persian labels and exercises build gradually.</p><p class="small">First manuscript edition · 2026<br>© 2026 A World in Alef. Personal study copy.<br>Illustrations created for this edition with AI assistance. Editorial and teaching review should precede commercial publication.</p>

<h2>Look. Try. Come back.</h2>
<h3>1 / Look</h3><p>Read the explanation and study the model. Persian runs from right to left. English guidance runs from left to right.</p><h3>2 / Try</h3><p>Do each numbered activity before checking pages 41–44. Writing tasks have model answers, not scores for artistic perfection.</p><h3>3 / Come back</h3><p>Cover a model and recall it later. Two attentive minutes count.</p><h3>Our sound guide</h3><p><b>â</b> represents the vowel in <i>âb</i>. <b>a, e, o</b> represent three short vowels. These are learning labels; English spelling cannot reproduce every Persian sound exactly.</p><p class="note">No audio is bundled yet. For pronunciation, use a fluent speaker’s model. You can still practise visual recognition and writing with this book.</p>
