<h2>Alef has a few jobs</h2>
<div class="reading" lang="fa" dir="rtl">ا &nbsp; آ</div><p>Meet <b>alef</b>. The shape with the mark on top, <span class="fa" lang="fa" dir="rtl">آ</span>, represents <b>â</b> at the beginning of our first word.</p><p>The plain shape <span class="fa" lang="fa" dir="rtl">ا</span> often represents â inside a word. At the beginning, it can instead support a short vowel. We will meet those short vowels on page 15.</p><p>These two shapes are taught together; the mark on <span class="fa" lang="fa" dir="rtl">آ</span> is not a dot.</p><div class="task"><strong>Try 04.</strong> Circle the shape that has the mark above it.
