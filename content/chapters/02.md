<h2>Start on the right</h2>
<p>Start a Persian word at its right-hand end. Move left. Begin the next line at the right again.</p><div class="direction">←</div><div class="reading" lang="fa" dir="rtl">آب</div><p>This is a visual model for direction; its meaning comes soon.</p><div class="task"><strong>Try 02.</strong> Point to the start of the model. Is it the tall shape on the right or the bowl-shaped letter on the left?</div><h3>Two directions can share a page</h3><p>Read these English instructions normally. Switch direction only when you enter a Persian example.</p>
