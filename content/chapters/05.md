<h2>A gap can live inside a word</h2>
<div class="reading" lang="fa" dir="rtl">باد</div><p>Be connects to alef. Alef does not connect to dâl. That small gap is still inside one word.</p><p>So far, both alef and dâl stop a connection to the following letter.
