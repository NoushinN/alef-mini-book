# A Little Picnic on a Windy Day

Sam brings water; Sara brings bread. As they spread their empty cloth in the courtyard, a gust carries it towards the wooden doorway. Sara catches one corner. They move the cloth into a sheltered spot beside the wall and share their picnic. The small interruption becomes a story of its own.

## Six story moments

1. Page 5: preparation; leaves begin to stir.
2. Page 13: the cloth takes flight; food and glassware stay safe.
3. Page 21: Sara catches the cloth at the doorway; they choose a new spot.
4. Page 31: they move and reset the picnic together.
5. Page 37: they share water and bread.
6. Page 40: the reflective ending after the reading challenge.
